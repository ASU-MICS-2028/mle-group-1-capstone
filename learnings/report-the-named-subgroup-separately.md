# Report the named subgroup separately

Prosit 1's bootstrap task asked about an *under-sampled rural district*. We computed intervals for all 16 regions and, because the survey had no district column, used the rural part of one region as a stand-in. We lost a point on that criterion. Our reading (no marker comments): the answer was buried in a broad table instead of being a clearly named subgroup with its own interval, its own sample size, and a sentence on why it was chosen.

What to do instead:
- Restate the brief's wording and name exactly which subgroup answers it.
- If the data can't give the exact subgroup, say so in one line and justify the substitute.
- Give that subgroup its own headline: estimate, CI, n, and how it compares to the national figure.
- Put the all-groups table after it, as support.

## For the capstone
The brief asks for fairness by region. Give the regions that matter most (for example the northern regions and any with very few reported floods) their own lines in the Model Card: recall, false-alarm rate, n flood-weeks and CI, not only a 16-row table.
