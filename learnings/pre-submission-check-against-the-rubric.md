# Pre-submission check against the rubric

Before Prosit 2 went in we checked it against the Prosit 1 grade, criterion by criterion. Steps that worked:

1. **Walk the rubric.** For each criterion, point to the exact section, table or file that answers it. A criterion with no clear pointer is a likely lost mark.
2. **Re-read the last grade's losses** and confirm each is fixed: reproducible from a fresh clone without a teammate; split both grouped and stratified (or say why not); the named subgroup reported on its own; every planning number sourced or flagged with sensitivity.
3. **Remove draft traces:** "DRAFT" markers, placeholder dates, "to come" notes, TODOs, comments meant for the team.
4. **Make claims consistent everywhere.** A changed decision must be updated in the README, ADRs, write-ups, Model Card and slides (we found one section still describing a superseded policy).
5. **Rerun tests and the notebooks** one last time, and confirm the numbers in the report match the saved tables.
6. **Disclose anything unusual** (e.g. the leaderboard probe, data breakdowns left out) as a limitation rather than hoping it goes unnoticed.

## For the capstone
Keep a `docs/rubric_check.md` table (criterion → where it's answered → who checked) and fill it two days before submission.
