# AI drafts must match the house format

In Prosit 2 an AI assistant drafted the report, a separate audit and a 15-slide deck from our saved results, in its own layout and voice. Two days later we rebuilt both: the deck as a 10-minute talk (11 slides plus 4 backups) and the report as a Word document (10 pages), on the Ashesi report and presentation templates, with the African-centred design the group uses, and saved to the shared Drive. The numbers didn't change; the time spent on the first drafts was mostly lost.

What we should have done first:
1. Open the previous submissions and the Ashesi templates before drafting anything.
2. Fix the format up front: template, page limit, slide count for a 10-minute debrief, file type (.docx/.pptx in Drive), readable large tables.
3. Draft *into* the template, section by section, with the owner of each part editing in their own words.
4. Treat AI text as a first draft that everyone must check and be able to defend. Reflections are written by each member, not by AI.

## For the capstone
Before the first draft of the report or deck, collect the Prosit 1 and 2 submissions and the templates, agree the outline and format in a short ADR, and only then generate content into it.
