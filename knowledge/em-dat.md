# EM-DAT

**What:** CRED's international disaster database, with event dates, locations and impacts (deaths, affected people).
**Link (terms):** https://doc.emdat.be/docs/legal/terms-of-use/
**Licence:** free for non-commercial use after registration.
**Status:** terms read; **no account yet**.

## For the capstone
Not a training label (it records only large disasters), but useful context for the Introduction and as a second check that major Groundsource events (e.g. June 2015, 2023 spillage) line up with recorded disasters.
