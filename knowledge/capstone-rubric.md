# Capstone rubric

The capstone is marked out of 100 and scaled to 40% of the final course grade.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| A. Problem framing | 10 |
| B. Presentation | 10 |
| C. Technical understanding | 20 |
| D. Technical report | 20 |
| E. Code submission | 20 |
| F. Deployment & usable artefact | 10 |
| G. Process & contribution | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Code (E) and the report (D) together are 40 points, so reproducibility and writing matter as much as modelling. "Technical understanding" (C) is tested live in the oral defence, where each member must explain the whole system without AI help. "Process & contribution" (G) draws on the contribution statements and the repo history.

The proposal's separate 20-point review is feedback only and not part of the 40%.

## For the capstone
Lesson from Prosit 1 (75/80): marks were lost on reproducibility, the split, the budget and A4 page format. These map onto E, C and D here.
