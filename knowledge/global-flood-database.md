# Global Flood Database

**What:** MODIS satellite-mapped extents of large flood events, 2000–2018.
**Links:** https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GLOBAL_FLOOD_DB_MODIS_EVENTS_V1 · paper: Tellman B et al. (2021), *Nature* 596, 80–86, doi:10.1038/s41586-021-03695-w (also shows the share of population exposed to floods is rising).
**Licence:** CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial).
**Status:** catalogue terms read.

## For the capstone
An independent, non-news label for 2015–2018. Compare which region-weeks it marks as flooded against Groundsource to estimate under-reporting by region — evidence for the news-bias audit and the mitigation. It only captures large, satellite-visible floods, so it misses flash floods in city drains.
