# Team roles

The brief asks teams to agree roles early (Week 10). Roles may overlap, but **every member must own something**:

- **Problem lead** — framing, decision, cost/benefit of errors.
- **Data lead** — acquisition, cleaning, leakage checks.
- **Modelling lead** — models and validation.
- **Ethics & communication lead** — Datasheet, Model Card, fairness analysis, reporting.

Ownership is not a shield: **each member must be able to explain the whole system**, because any member can be questioned on any part in the defence. In the presentation every member speaks and owns a section.

## For the capstone
For the flood project the data role is the heaviest (joining Groundsource, CHIRPS, ERA5-Land and terrain into one region-week table), so consider splitting it or pairing the modelling lead on it until Week 11.
