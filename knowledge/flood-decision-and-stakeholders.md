# Flood decision and stakeholders

The brief demands a real decision-maker who would act differently with the model. The clearest ones:

- **NADMO regional offices** — position staff and relief supplies ahead of likely flooding; issue public warnings. Primary user of the weekly regional dashboard.
- **Metropolitan, municipal and district assemblies** — time drain clearing and public warnings; for the land-change add-on, target drainage, wetland protection and building enforcement.
- Secondary: ambulance services and health facilities (access during floods), Ghana Meteorological Agency and Hydrological Authority (data and forecasts).

**Status: none has been consulted.** The report recommends contacting NADMO, GMet, the Hydrological Authority or an assembly, and **not naming them in deliverables as partners unless they agree**. A conversation would strengthen framing, costs and the explainer.

The tool supports, not replaces, their judgement. Flooding in Accra is also structural (informal urbanisation, drainage), so the model gives evidence about where and when pressure is greatest; it cannot unblock a drain.
