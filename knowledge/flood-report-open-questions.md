# Flood report: unverified items and open questions

**Not verified (per the report):**
- Events were assigned to regions by **polygon centre**, so large multi-region events count for one region only; the 16-region boundaries were **simplified**. Rates would shift with another method.
- Ghana events have **not been hand-checked**; the joined region-week table has **not been built**; **no model has been trained** — no results exist yet.
- Citation details were confirmed via Crossref, but **not every paper was read in full**. Read each before relying on its methods.
- Casualty and displacement figures come from **press reports** and vary; check primary sources (NADMO, IFRC).
- Licence notes come from catalogue pages; re-read before submission. "Not confirmed": CHIRPS-GEFS, GloFAS, geoBoundaries.
- Whether Flood Hub or other services already cover **urban rain flooding in Accra** is open.
- **No search yet for existing uses of Groundsource in Ghana** — needed before claiming novelty.
- Groundsource precision for Ghana unknown (75% in one abstract; higher elsewhere).
- Local data (Ghana Meteorological Agency stations, Hydrological Authority, NADMO incident records): licence and access unknown; would improve rainfall and give independent labels — ask the agencies.

**Team questions:** who has time for the data joining? All Ghana or Greater Accra first? Can anyone reach NADMO, GMet, the Hydrological Authority or an assembly? Are we willing to say throughout that the model predicts *reported* floods?
