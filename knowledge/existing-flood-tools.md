# Existing flood tools and studies

| Effort | What it does | Gap it leaves |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Flood Hub** — Nearing G et al. (2024), *Nature* 627, 559–563, doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07145-1; African rollout (Mail & Guardian, May 2023, https://mg.co.za/environment/2023-05-23-google-expands-flood-alerts-to-more-african-countries/) | AI riverine flood forecasts up to 7 days ahead; Ghana covered | Targets river floods; whether it covers rain-driven urban drain flooding in Accra is **an open question to check** |
| **GloFAS** (Copernicus EMS) — https://ewds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/cems-glofas-historical | Global river discharge forecasts and history | Coarse, river-based; an input, not a local decision tool. Licence not confirmed; account likely needed |
| **Ghana susceptibility studies** — Forson ED et al. (2022), frequency ratio, Greater Accra, doi:10.1007/s40808-022-01616-y; Ghansah B et al. (2021), random forest, Nasia watershed, doi:10.1080/23311916.2021.1923384 | Static maps of flood-prone areas | Show *where*, not *when* to act |
| **Satellite flood mapping** — Amoah GO (2026), Sentinel-1 SAR, Lower Volta, doi:10.1016/j.geomat.2026.100131; Sen1Floods11, Bonafilia D et al. (2020), doi:10.1109/cvprw50498.2020.00113 | Flood extent during/after events | No warning ahead of time |

Our niche: a **weekly, regional, rain-driven** warning covering all 16 regions, including urban flash floods, evaluated forward in time. The team has **not yet searched for existing uses of Groundsource in Ghana** — do this before claiming novelty.
