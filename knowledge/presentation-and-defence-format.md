# Presentation and defence format

Week 15 (14–18 Dec 2026). About **15 minutes of presentation plus 8–10 minutes of questions** per group. **Every member must speak and own a section.**

| Slot | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 min | The problem and the decision |
| 2 | 3 min | Data and features |
| 3 | 4 min | Models, validation and results, with diagnostics |
| 4 | 3 min | Ethics, fairness and regulatory review |
| 5 | 2 min | The artefact, the stakeholder explainer, and limitations |
| 6 | 1 min | Next steps; contributions |

The slides must end with the AI-use declaration. The defence is where understanding is checked: any member may be asked about any part, and answers must come without AI assistance.

**Lecturer emphasis (lecture 9).** He allowed AI in the project precisely because he plans to "drill" each member in the presentation: expect probing questions on why every step was done, not just what was done.

## For the capstone
Slot 3 should show the temporal-split results against the rainfall-threshold baseline; slot 4 the per-region results and rural under-reporting; slot 5 a live dashboard demo with the "reported floods, not actual risk" caveat.
