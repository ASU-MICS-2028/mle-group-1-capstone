# Population, sample, parameter, statistic

| Term | Meaning | Bed-net example |
|---|---|---|
| Population | the whole group we care about | all households in Ghana |
| Sample | the part we actually observe | 17,933 surveyed households |
| Parameter | a true fact about the population (unknown) | the real national ownership rate |
| Statistic | a number computed from the sample | 70.96% |

We use the statistic to estimate the parameter. A different sample would give a slightly different statistic, so we must also say how far off it might be (a standard error or confidence interval).

The lecture's second guiding question: *how much should we trust the numbers we compute?* We never see the whole population.

## For the capstone
Our 2015–2025 region-weeks are a sample of Ghana's flood history, and our test score is a statistic: it estimates how the model would do on future weeks in general, with uncertainty.
