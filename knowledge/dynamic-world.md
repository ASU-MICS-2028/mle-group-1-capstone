# Dynamic World

**What:** Google/WRI 10 m land-cover classification from Sentinel-2, from 2015, updated continuously.
**Link:** https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GOOGLE_DYNAMICWORLD_V1
**Licence:** CC BY 4.0.
**Status:** terms read.

## For the capstone
Annual land-cover shares per region (built, trees, crops, bare, water) and their change since 2015 — the finer-grained complement to GHSL for the land-change question. Cloud cover in the rainy season can make composites noisy; use annual or dry-season composites.
