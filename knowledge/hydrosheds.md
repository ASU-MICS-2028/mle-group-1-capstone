# HydroSHEDS

**What:** Global river networks and catchment boundaries (Earth Engine "HydroSHEDS Free Flowing Rivers").
**Link:** https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/WWF_HydroSHEDS_v1_FreeFlowingRivers
**Licence:** free for commercial and non-commercial use.
**Status:** terms read.

## For the capstone
River density or distance-to-river per region as a static feature. Also used in the galamsey fallback (distance to water).
