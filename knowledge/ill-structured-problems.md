# Ill-structured problems

The course is taught through problem-based learning (PBL). The lecturer explained that prosits are **ill-structured problems**: there is no single correct answer and the brief does not tell you every step.

His suggested order:
1. Read until you really understand the problem and its question.
2. Then analyse the data.
3. Then add your own ideas (innovation).

He keeps lectures short (under about 1h45) because lecture-style ML is freely available online; the value of the course is building things. He also asked groups to plan daily goals.

Since there is no single right answer, marks go to how well you justify your choices, not to matching a hidden solution.

## For the capstone
Write down our choices and why (label definition, temporal split, alert threshold) as decision records, so we can justify them in the defence.
