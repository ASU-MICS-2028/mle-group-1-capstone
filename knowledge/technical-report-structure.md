# Technical report structure

About **8–12 pages, PDF**, structured as a research paper. Required sections, in this order:

1. Abstract
2. Introduction
3. Related work (LO7: ground 3–5 methodological choices in the literature)
4. Problem formulation & cost matrix (LO1; the brief calls this "Part A")
5. Data & pipeline
6. Methods, **with derivations** (LO3: at least two methods you actually use)
7. Results & diagnostics (LO4: metrics, diagnostic plots, honest validation, uncertainty)
8. Discussion, including the ethical & fairness review (LO5)
9. Limitations
10. Conclusion
11. Reproducibility statement
12. AI-use declaration
13. References
14. Appendix

A first full draft is expected at the Week 13 clinic (Fri 4 Dec); a draft goes in with the repo in Week 14; the final version is submitted in Week 15.

## For the capstone
The cost matrix should compare a missed flood (unprepared region) with a false alert (wasted preparation, alert fatigue). Results should include the rainfall-rule baseline, per-region breakdowns, and the leakage-free vs forecast-rain comparison.
