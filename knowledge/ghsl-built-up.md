# GHSL built-up surface

**What:** Global Human Settlement Layer built-up surface, in five-year steps from 1975 (some later epochs are projections).
**Link:** https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/JRC_GHSL_P2023A_GHS_BUILT_S
**Licence:** open reuse with attribution.
**Status:** terms read.

## For the capstone
Built-up share per region and its growth: the core input for the land-change question. Five-year steps are coarse; interpolate between epochs and avoid projected epochs where observed data exist. Possible **proxy for media attention** — test it.
