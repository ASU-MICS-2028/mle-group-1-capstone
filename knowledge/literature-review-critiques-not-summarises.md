# Literature reviews critique, not summarise

The lecturer was clear that a review which only summarises papers is not a proper review. A real review **critiques**: what are the limitations, what could the authors have done better, what did they not consider?

His picture: a wall with one brick missing. Your job is to find the missing brick, i.e. the research gap.

His tip for entering a new field: start with existing review papers, then read the "limitations" and "future work" sections of key papers. That is where gaps are listed.

## For the capstone
When we cite flood-forecasting work in "related work", say what each paper could not do (e.g. no Ghana data, no weekly scale, labels from gauges not news) and how our project addresses or inherits that gap.
