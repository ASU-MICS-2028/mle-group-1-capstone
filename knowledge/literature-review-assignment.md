# Literature review assignment

An individual task, separate from the capstone, worth **10%**.

- Write a **structured critical review** of current literature on one classical ML topic.
- At least **10 peer-reviewed sources** in total. Of these, **at least 3** must be discussed and compared in depth (method, data, validation, metrics, baselines, tuning, generalisability).
- Reviewed papers should be from the **last 10 years (2016 on)**. Older seminal papers may still be cited.
- Document a **reproducible search strategy** (see the related note).
- Use the provided conference template (Word or LaTeX), **IEEE** referencing, one PDF on Canvas.
- Structure: title and author block, ~100-word abstract, introduction, search strategy, comparison of contributions, methodological critique, synthesis (why findings conflict and where you stand), gaps, conclusion, references.
- 16 topics in 4 themes, one per student, first come first served: class imbalance and rare events; validation, data quality and model maintenance; fairness and interpretability; model selection on small or sparse data.
- No papers are supplied; you find them.
- Due date was announced as the 18th (November); students later reported confusion between the 16th and the 18th. Check Canvas.

The Writing Centre can review drafts. There is no presentation, but the lecturer may call you to defend work that does not sound like you.

## For the capstone
The imbalance theme overlaps directly with our flood problem (rare events, early warning). A review on threshold tuning vs resampling would feed our modelling choices.
