# Capstone proposal requirements

Due Fri 6 Nov 2026, 23:59 GMT. Kept here because the approved proposal is the scope we are held to.

**Format:** exactly one page (A4 or Letter), single-spaced, 11–12 pt, ~2.54 cm margins; PDF only (no .docx); one per group listing every member; file name `ICS553_FinalProposal_<GroupName>.pdf`; submitted on the LMS and a copy kept in the repo; ends with a one-line AI-use declaration; late penalty −25%/day, not accepted after 2 days.

**Content, in order:** header block (title, group, members, date, 2–3 sentence summary); 1 Background (~80 words, 1–2 sources); 2 Problem to solve (~120 words: who decides what and when, label, error costs, task type); 3 How you will solve it (~150 words: data with source/size/licence, features and leakage risks, 3–5 models, split and metrics, artefact); 4 Feasibility (~150 words).

**Feasibility checklist (every line answered):** data availability, quality and labels, licence/privacy/ethics, compute, time, skills and support, evaluation feasibility, African context fit, risks and fallback.

**Outcomes:** Approved (held to scope); Approved with revisions (apply them); Revise & resubmit (within 48 h, no penalty); Not approved (meet instructor). Review rubric (20 pts, feedback only, not in the 40%): clarity 5, technical soundness 5 (incl. two derivations named, leakage anticipated), feasibility 6, African impact 2, communication 2.

## For the capstone
Our submission presented three options and asked for guidance; Option B was approved on 9 Nov 2026. Keep the final PDF in the repo as the brief asks.

**Lecture-9 extras.** The lecturer said the proposal itself is ungraded (feedback only), must be written in prose paragraphs rather than bullet points, and that the feasibility table is mainly for the team's own benefit: by filling it in you confirm the data and resources really exist.
