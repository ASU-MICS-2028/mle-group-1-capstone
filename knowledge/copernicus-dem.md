# Copernicus DEM (GLO-30)

**What:** Global 30 m digital elevation model.
**Link:** https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_DEM_GLO30
**Licence:** free for this use, with attribution.
**Status:** terms read.

## For the capstone
Static per-region terrain features: mean and spread of elevation and slope, share of low-lying land. Static features cannot explain *when* floods happen, but they shift baseline risk between regions (and the F3 fallback relies on them heavily).
