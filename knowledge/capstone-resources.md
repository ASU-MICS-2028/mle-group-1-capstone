# Capstone resources (from the brief)

**Course textbooks:** ISLR (James et al.), HOMLP (Géron), DLB (Goodfellow et al., *Deep Learning*), FML (Barocas, Hardt & Narayanan), Molnar's *Interpretable Machine Learning*.

**Must-read papers:** Gebru et al. (2021) Datasheets for Datasets; Mitchell et al. (2019) Model Cards for Model Reporting; Buolamwini & Gebru (2018) Gender Shades.

**Tools:** Python with pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost/LightGBM, imbalanced-learn, shap; PyTorch/TensorFlow; OpenCV; Google Colab or Kaggle kernels (free GPU); GitHub; joblib/Streamlit/Gradio for artefacts.

**African data starting points** (licence and access must still be checked): Zindi (https://zindi.africa/competitions); World Bank Microdata / Global Findex; DHS Program (https://dhsprogram.com); Ghana Open Data (https://data.gov.gh) and the Ghana Statistical Service; WHO / Africa CDC; Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX); OpenStreetMap, Sentinel-2, NASA and ESA imagery; Masakhane and other African NLP corpora.

## For the capstone
HDX hosts the Ghana administrative boundaries (CC BY-IGO); OpenStreetMap supplies roads and facilities for an exposure layer.
