# JRC Global Surface Water

**What:** Landsat-based history of surface water occurrence and change, 1984–2021.
**Links:** https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/JRC_GSW1_4_GlobalSurfaceWater · paper: Pekel JF et al. (2016), *Nature* 540, 418–422, doi:10.1038/nature20584.
**Licence:** free, with acknowledgement.
**Status:** terms read.

## For the capstone
Static feature: share of each region that is often or seasonally wet (floodplains, lagoons). Coverage stops in 2021, so treat it as static, not as a time-varying input.
