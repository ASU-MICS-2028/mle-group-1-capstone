# Capstone timeline

| Week | Date (2026) | Milestone | Expected state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Fri 6 Nov | Project starts; proposal due | One-page PDF submitted |
| 10 | 9–13 Nov | — | Data acquisition under way; team roles final |
| 11 | **Fri 20 Nov** | **Mid-check with instructor** | **Data in hand, EDA done, baseline model running** |
| 12 | 23–27 Nov | — | Feature engineering; **≥ 4 models compared** |
| 13 | Fri 4 Dec | Final Project Clinic | Validation, fairness analysis, first full report draft |
| 14 | **Fri 11 Dec** | **Code repository due** | Repo, report draft, ethics pack, artefact complete |
| 15 | 14–18 Dec | Final presentations | Slides + live defence; **final report submitted** |

Late work loses 25% per day and is not accepted after two days. Week 15 falls in the exam period; the syllabus lists the capstone as the course's examination.

**Flood-specific targets** (from the options report): Week 10 — Earth Engine accounts, label table, hand-audit of ~100 events, roles, contact NADMO; Week 11 — region-week table, EDA, baselines and logistic regression running; Week 12 — land-change features and ≥ 4 models; Week 13 — temporal validation and fairness audit.

## For the capstone
The region-week table is the critical path. Fix it by the Week 11 mid-check and add land features only after the baseline works.

**Lecture-9 detail.** Because the course has no written exam, no venue is assigned: once the exam timetable is out, groups **schedule their own presentation**, in exam week or the week before if preferred. The mid-check is a few minutes in class to confirm data, EDA and baselines.
